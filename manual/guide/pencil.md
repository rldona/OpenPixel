# Apple Pencil and gestures

OpenPixel is made for the Apple Pencil, and your fingers handle everything else.

| Gesture | Action |
|---|---|
| Apple Pencil | Draw with the current tool. |
| One finger | Draws until you use the Pencil; after that, fingers only navigate (you can change this in Preferences). |
| Pinch / two fingers | Zoom and pan. |
| Two-finger tap | Undo. |
| Three-finger tap | Redo. |
| Touch and hold with a finger | Quick eyedropper (while you draw with the Pencil). |
| Four-finger tap | Focus mode: hide or show the panels. |
| Pencil double tap | Switch between the eraser and the previous tool. |
| Squeeze (Apple Pencil Pro) | Swap the primary and secondary colors. |

What the double tap and the squeeze do can be changed in [Preferences](./preferences): the system setting, the eraser, swap colors, the eyedropper, undo, or nothing.

## Pressure

The tool options can make Pencil **pressure** change the brush size, the opacity, or both.

## Focus mode

The arrows button in the top bar (or ⇥ Tab, or a four-finger tap) hides the panels and leaves only the canvas, with a small capsule to play and scrub the animation. Its button leaves focus mode.

## Zoom

Pinch to zoom; the zoom snaps to whole levels so pixels stay square. The side panel's **100%**, **Center** and **Fit screen** buttons (⌘1, ⌘0) set it at once.
