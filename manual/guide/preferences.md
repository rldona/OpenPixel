## General

- **Language**: English or Spanish.
- **Appearance**: system, light or dark.
- **Accent color**: ember, blue, green, purple or pink.
- **Layout**: tools on the right and the color panel on the left, for left-handed drawing (like Aseprite's mirrored layout).

## Editor

After [Aseprite's preferences](https://www.aseprite.org/docs/preferences/):

- **Alpha and opacity** shown as 0–255 or as a percentage.
- **Rewind on stop**: stopping playback goes back to the frame it started from.
- **Undo steps**: more steps use more memory with big sprites.
- **Transparent background**: the checkerboard's size (automatic keeps the squares a similar size on screen at any zoom) and its two colors.
- **Grid**: the colors of the pixel grid and the tile grid. The color's opacity is the grid's opacity.

## Apple Pencil and touch

- **Finger**: draws until the Pencil is used (the default), always draws, or only navigates. Two fingers always move and zoom the canvas.
- **Double tap** and **Squeeze**: what the Pencil gestures do. See [Apple Pencil and gestures](./pencil).

## More

- **Keyboard shortcuts**: the full list. See [Keyboard shortcuts](./shortcuts).
- **Extensions** and **Scripts**: install, see and remove them. See [Scripts and extensions](./scripts).
- **Reset editor preferences** puts the editor settings back; language, appearance and accent color are kept.
